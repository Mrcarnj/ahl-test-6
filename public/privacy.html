AHL Officials

Privacy Policy

AHL Officials · Effective October 1, 2026

AHL Officials ("the App") is an app for American Hockey League officials, available on iOS, Android and the web at ahl-test-6.expo.app. It is run by Mike Dietrich ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles, why, and what you can do about it.

Who can use the App

Accounts are created by the App's administrator for AHL officials. There is no public sign-up. The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

Information we hold

Account and profile. Your name, email address, phone number and headshot photo, which the administrator adds when your account is created. Your password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider; we never see it.

Your schedule feed. The personal HorizonWebRef calendar (iCal) link you enter, and the game assignments the App reads from it (games, dates, times, arenas, and the officials assigned).

Onboarding records. Whether you have changed your initial password, and when you accepted the Terms of Service.

Notifications. If you allow notifications on a phone, that device's push-notification token and its type (iOS or Android).

On your device. The App stores your sign-in session and cached copies of your schedule, the roster and team data on your device (in the browser's local storage on the web) so it loads quickly and works with a weak connection. This data stays on your device and is removed when you sign out or clear the App's data.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

How the information is used

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show your game schedule, crew, arenas, team rosters and standings.
  • To notify you, and the rest of your crew, when a game you are assigned to changes.
  • To let officials contact one another.

Who can see your information

  • Other signed-in AHL officials can see your name, email, phone number, photo and game assignments, so crews can coordinate. Your HorizonWebRef calendar link is private to you.
  • No one else. Nothing is visible without signing in, and access is enforced by the database itself.

Services the App relies on

These providers process data only to run the App:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and file storage (United States).
  • Expo (650 Industries) — web hosting and delivery of push notifications.
  • Cloudflare — the proxy that fetches your calendar feed on the web.
  • HorizonWebRef — your calendar feed is fetched from HorizonWebRef using the link you provide.
  • HockeyTech — public league statistics and standings are downloaded from HockeyTech; none of your information is sent to it.
  • Google Maps — arena maps on the web are embedded from Google Maps, which is subject to Google's privacy policy.

Cookies

The App itself does not set cookies. Your sign-in session is kept in your browser's local storage, which is needed for the App to work. The embedded Google Map may set its own cookies when it loads.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information while you are an active official with an account. Ask us to delete your account at any time and we will remove your profile, calendar link and push tokens. Game records that list you as an assigned official may be kept as part of the league schedule history.

Your choices and rights

You can view and update your calendar link in the App, turn notifications off in your device settings, and sign out at any time. To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email mike.f.dietrich@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Data is protected by row-level access rules in the database, so each request only returns what that signed-in official is allowed to see. Sign-in is protected by rate limiting. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, let officials know in the App.

Contact

Questions or requests: mike.f.dietrich@gmail.com
