Support

AHL Officials is the schedule, roster and rules app for American Hockey League officials, available on iOS, Android and the web. If something isn't working or you have a question, we're here to help.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get an account?

Accounts are created by the App's administrator for AHL officials; there is no public sign-up. If you are an AHL official and don't have an account, email us from the address you'd like to sign in with.

I forgot my password or can't sign in.

Email us and we'll reset it. After several failed attempts the sign-in screen asks you to wait briefly before trying again — that's a security measure, not a problem with your account.

My games aren't showing up.

The App reads your assignments from your personal HorizonWebRef calendar (iCal) link. Check it under Profile → iCal URL: it should be the subscription link from HorizonWebRef, starting with https:// or webcal://. The schedule refreshes when you open the App; new or changed assignments can take a few minutes to appear after they are posted in HorizonWebRef.

I'm not getting game-change notifications.

Go to Profile → Enable Notifications, then make sure notifications for AHL Officials are allowed in your device's Settings. Push notifications are not available in the web version; changes still appear there live while the page is open.

Rosters or stats look out of date.

Rosters, statistics and standings come from the league's public data and update once a day. At the start of a season they stay empty until the league publishes them.

How do I delete my account?

Email mike.f.dietrich@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask for it to be deleted. We will remove your profile, calendar link and push-notification tokens within 30 days and confirm by email. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How is my information used?

See the Privacy Policy. In short: your details are visible only to other signed-in AHL officials, your calendar link is private to you, and the App has no ads, analytics or tracking.

Report a problem

Email us with what you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot helps a lot.
